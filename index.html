KhaoJi - Privacy Policy & Terms

Effective Date: October 2024 | App Name: KhaoJi

Welcome to KhaoJi. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This privacy policy informs you about how we handle your personal data when you use our mobile application and outlines your privacy rights.

1. Information We Collect

To provide fast food delivery, shop ordering, and staff dispatch services, KhaoJi collects the following data:

  • Personal Identification: Name, Email Address, Phone Number.
  • Delivery Information: Delivery Addresses, Latitude/Longitude coordinates.
  • Location Data (Riders & Customers): Foreground & Background location during active deliveries for real-time tracking and geofencing verification.
  • Device & Technical Data: Unique Device ID, IP Address, App Crash Logs (Firebase Crashlytics), and Analytics events.
  • Push Notification Tokens: FCM Tokens to send live order status updates.

2. How We Use Your Data

  • To process and deliver your food/grocery orders safely.
  • To verify rider presence near delivery locations (100m Geofence).
  • To prevent fraud, duplicate orders, and promo code abuse.
  • To send real-time push notifications regarding order progress.

3. Third-Party Services We Use

KhaoJi uses trusted Google/Firebase infrastructure for authentication, database, analytics, and maps:

  • Firebase Authentication & Firestore: Account management & Database.
  • Google Maps SDK & Geocoding API: Location display and address lookup.
  • Firebase Crashlytics & Analytics: App stability monitoring and performance tracking.
  • Firebase App Check (Play Integrity): Fraud & bot prevention.

4. Data Security

We implement strict security measures, including Role-Based Access Control (RBAC), SSL/TLS Encryption, Data Masking (Phone numbers masked as 0300*****67), and Code Obfuscation to protect your data from unauthorized access.

5. Account & Data Deletion Request (Google Policy 2024)

In compliance with Google Play Policies, you have the right to request the deletion of your account and associated personal data at any time.

How to Request Account & Data Deletion:

  1. In-App: Go to Profile Settings ➔ Tap Edit Profile or Delete Account.
  2. Via Email: Send an email from your registered email address to khaojiofficial@gmail.com with the subject line "ACCOUNT DELETION REQUEST".

Upon receiving your request, your personal data (Name, Phone, Email, Saved Addresses) will be permanently deleted from our active databases within 7 business days, except for transaction/audit records required for tax and legal compliance.

6. Terms & Conditions

  • Orders once placed have a 30-second grace cancellation window.
  • Cash on Delivery (COD) orders must be paid upon arrival to the rider.
  • Abuse of order cancellations or fake OTP requests will result in account suspension.
