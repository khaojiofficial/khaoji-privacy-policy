Privacy Policy & Terms of Service

Effective date: October 2024 App: KhaoJi

Welcome to KhaoJi. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This policy explains how we handle your data when you use our mobile application, and what rights you have.

Data we collect How we use it Third-party services Security Account deletion Terms Contact

1Information we collect

To provide food delivery, shop ordering and staff dispatch services, KhaoJi collects the following data:

  • Personal identification: name, email address, phone number.
  • Delivery information: delivery addresses and latitude/longitude coordinates.
  • Location data (riders and customers): foreground and background location during active deliveries, for real-time tracking and geofencing verification.
  • Device and technical data: unique device ID, IP address, app crash logs (Firebase Crashlytics) and analytics events.
  • Push notification tokens: FCM tokens used to send live order status updates.

2How we use your data

  • To process and deliver your food and grocery orders safely.
  • To verify rider presence near delivery locations (100 m geofence).
  • To prevent fraud, duplicate orders and promo code abuse.
  • To send real-time push notifications about order progress.

3Third-party services we use

KhaoJi relies on trusted Google and Firebase infrastructure for authentication, database, analytics and maps:

  • Firebase Authentication and Firestore: account management and database.
  • Google Maps SDK and Geocoding API: location display and address lookup.
  • Firebase Crashlytics and Analytics: app stability monitoring and performance tracking.
  • Firebase App Check (Play Integrity): fraud and bot prevention.

4Data security

We apply strict security measures to protect your data from unauthorized access, including role-based access control (RBAC), SSL/TLS encryption, data masking (for example, phone numbers shown as 0300*****67) and code obfuscation.

5Account and data deletion

In line with Google Play policy, you can request deletion of your account and associated personal data at any time.

How to request deletion

  1. In the app: go to Profile Settings, then tap Delete Account.
  2. By email: write from your registered email address to khaojiofficial@gmail.com with the subject ACCOUNT DELETION REQUEST.

After we receive your request, your personal data (name, phone, email, saved addresses) is permanently deleted from our active databases within 7 business days. Transaction and audit records required for tax and legal compliance may be retained.

6Terms and conditions

  • Orders have a 30-second grace window in which they can be cancelled after placing.
  • Cash on Delivery (COD) orders must be paid to the rider on arrival.
  • Abuse of order cancellations or fake OTP requests will result in account suspension.
